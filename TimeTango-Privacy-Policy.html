Privacy Policy for TimeTango

Last updated: 21 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TimeTango ("TimeTango", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use the TimeTango mobile application (the "App"). TimeTango is developed and operated in England, and this policy is written to comply with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

TimeTango is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at support@time-tango.app and we will delete it.

1. Who We Are

TimeTango is a social scheduling app that helps you and your friends or groups find mutual free time and coordinate plans. We are the data controller responsible for your personal information in connection with your use of the App.

Data Controller Contact
Email: support@time-tango.app

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide to us

  • Account information: email address, password (stored securely, hashed), and username.
  • Profile information: full name (optional) and any accent/theme colour preference you set.
  • Availability information: the dates and time blocks (morning, afternoon, evening) you mark as free, and who you choose to share that availability with (everyone, friends, specific groups, or custom).
  • Social connections: friend connections you make via share codes, friend requests, and the groups you create or join.
  • Events you create: event titles, dates, time blocks, and any banner image you upload.
  • Meet requests: proposed and confirmed meeting times between you and friends or groups, and any message you attach to a request.
  • Communications: if you contact us for support, we collect the information you provide in that correspondence.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device calendar data (with your permission): if you choose to sync a device calendar, TimeTango reads which days contain existing events so it can mark them as "busy" on your TimeTango calendar. We only read whether a day is busy — we do not read, store, or transmit the titles, locations, attendees, or content of your calendar events. This calendar access happens locally on your device via Apple's EventKit framework; TimeTango does not upload your device calendar data to our servers.
  • Push notification tokens: if you enable notifications, we store a device token so we can deliver notifications about friend requests, meet requests, confirmations, and mutual free time matches.
  • Usage and log data: basic technical data such as app version and error logs, used to keep the App running reliably.

2.3 Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect precise location data.
  • We do not access your device contacts.
  • We do not read the contents of your device calendar events — only whether a day is busy.

4. Who Can See Your Information

TimeTango is a social app, so some of your information is, by design, visible to other users:

  • Availability blocks are visible only to the audience you choose when setting them (everyone using the App, your friends, a specific group, or a custom list of people).
  • Events you create may be visible to people you've granted access to, per the event's visibility settings.
  • Your username is visible to friends and group members so they can identify you.
  • Meet proposals and confirmations are visible to the other participant(s) in that meet.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not use your data for advertising.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your profile data from our systems. Some information may be retained for a limited period where required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

7. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data (e.g. via Profile Settings in the App).
  • Delete your account and associated data (via Profile Settings, or by contacting us).
  • Restrict or object to certain processing of your data.
  • Data portability — request a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent (e.g. calendar sync or push notifications), without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@time-tango.app. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK's data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

8. Account & Data Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Profile Settings within the App. This removes your profile and signs you out. If you would like your full account, including your authentication record, permanently deleted, please email support@time-tango.app and we will process your request.

9. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), access controls, and row-level security policies on our database so that users can only access data they're authorised to see. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

TimeTango is not directed at, and is not intended for use by, anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.
